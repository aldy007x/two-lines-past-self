Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Two Lines to Your Past Self"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca): two lines — a warning to my past self about one specific moment, and one sentence on why that moment mattered.

## The entry

> Don't send that 2 a.m. text to her on the night of 14 March, the one that starts "we need to talk".
>
> You lost the friendship you spent three years rebuilding, and the silence after it taught you that some doors only open once.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the
two-line entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `./output/`) and
self-checks the competition gate before exiting: UTF-8 text of 1–280 characters,
exactly two non-blank lines, each a complete sentence.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat output/entry.txt
```
